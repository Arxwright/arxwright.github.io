Support

How can we help?

Need help with Azimarc, or anything else from Arxwright? Email us directly. A real person reads every message.

support@arxwright.com

Replies can take up to three business days.

What to include

  • The product (for example, Azimarc) and the device or browser you’re using.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • Screenshots, if they help.

Never send your password. We will never ask for it.

Your account and data

To ask about your account or the data we hold, including deleting it, email us from the address your account uses.

Everything else

For anything that isn’t support: contact@arxwright.com.
